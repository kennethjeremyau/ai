# Unslop

Edit text to remove AI patterns and sound more human.

## Process
1. Look for patterns
2. Rewrite while preserving meaning

## Patterns

### Punctuation
1. **Em dash overuse.** Avoid em dashes entirely and use periods or commas instead.
2. **Semi-colon overuse.** Similar to em dash overuse.
3. **Colon overuse.** Fine before a list or example, but not as a mid-sentence connector.
4. **Boldface overuse.** Avoid bolding nouns or phrases mid-sentence.
5. **Curly quotes.** Replace with straight quotes.

### Content
1. **Signifying importance.** "Load-bearing", "carries the real weight", "smoking gun". Better to just get to the point.
2. **Plain language.** "use" instead of "utilize". Avoid synonyms if a simpler, clearer word is available.
3. **Not just X, but Y.** State the point directly instead.
